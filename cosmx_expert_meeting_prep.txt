A. OUR OWN PREP (before the call)

Reuven & Avinoam walk us through the workflow so we ask informed questions:
- Panel = Mouse 1K Universal Cell Panel (deliberate, for immune/stromal TME —
  not Neuroscience).
- Morphology kit channels (DAPI, PanCK, Membrane, CD45, "G") + segmentation
  algo (AtoMx v6, image-based).
- How FOVs were chosen (we skipped artifact regions -> discontinuous grid).
- The 8 custom probes — target + purpose of each.

The 8 custom probes: GFP (Cx3cr1 reporter), tdTomato (MDM reporter), TMEM119,
Trem2, Lyve1, GFAP, Ccl2, Cxcl13. (Cx3cr1 itself is standard panel, not custom.)

Evidence we bring (prepare 1–2 slides):
1. GFP is a dead probe — S/N ~1.73 vs GFAP control 12.5; only 42% of counts
   above background.
2. GFP vs Cx3cr1 negative correlation (r ~ -0.5 all slices) — expected positive.
3. tdTomato paradox — higher in microglia than MDMs in every slice (opposite of
   expected).
4. Lyve1 sparse/non-specific — brain-wide, not perivascular; low dynamic range.
5. Segmentation is not the cause — our re-seg test moved nothing (purity
   0.802->0.802; foreign transcripts ~2% median).
6. Ceiling is specific — astro/ependymal separate cleanly; microglia vs
   macrophage near chance (AUC ~0.59).

B. QUESTIONS FOR THE EXPERTS

Custom probes
1. Give us a per-probe performance table (counts, % above background, S/N) for
   all 8 across the 6 tissues — not just "most look fine."
2. GFP is at background (S/N 1.73). Do you agree it failed, and does the
   manufacturing log explain it?
3. Your report says "Ccl22" — our custom target is Ccl2. Which was actually made
   and detected?
4. Beyond intracellular localization, what evidence shows each probe binds its
   intended target (off-target/cross-hyb checks)?

Reporter transgenes
5. In your experience, how often is GFP mRNA undetectable in CosMx despite clear
   GFP protein? A base rate would help a lot.
6. GFP–Cx3cr1 is negatively correlated. Given GFP looks failed, is this an
   artifact — and can we salvage any myeloid internal control?
7. Any recommended orthogonal validation (IF/protein) to anchor microglia-vs-MDM
   identity?

Segmentation & FOV quality
8. What quantitative criteria flag a low-quality FOV?
9. Should we exclude poor FOVs?
11. Is full re-segmentation (Baysor/Cellpose/proseg/SAM) worth it here? Our export
    has the label-mask TIFFs (CellLabels/CompartmentLabels) but not the raw
    multichannel morphology images — only lossy JPG composites. Can you provide
    the raw Morphology2D TIFFs?
12. Anything that can be done at the image level where membrane or nuclei signal
    is too weak to segment (restaining, image enhancement)?
13. On slide 2 (L3-21) only 70.7% of transcripts are intracellular (~30%
    extracellular). Is that within spec, and how far should we trust per-cell
    counts there?

Imaging / H&E
14. What is the recommended workflow to project our H&E images to the CosMx
    FOV/cell coordinates, and which coordinate space should we align to?

Trust boundary
15. Since non-tumor segmentation can't improve much, can we still trust the tumor
    + TME results (our actual focus)? Yes / with caveats.
16. High counts in cerebellar grey matter — is our density+panel interpretation
    reasonable?

Spillover & BAMs
17. You attribute high GFAP to astrocyte processes bleeding into neighboring cells.
    Microglia and neurons are branched the same way, so their process transcripts
    should leak into neighbors too and corrupt the very cells we classify. How is
    this process-derived signal handled in brain CosMx studies?
18. Which multi-marker BAM definition do you recommend on this panel?

Annotation / reference
19. Given ~20% panel overlap with your brain profile, is InSituType expected to
    beat our current SingleR for microglia/MDM/BAM, or does it hit the same
    ceiling?
20. Do you agree the UCC panel is informationally insufficient to finely split
    microglia vs macrophage, or do you know a method that recovers it?
21. Tips for building/validating our own scRNA-seq reference on the UCC gene space.
22. Given the process-derived spillover (Q17), what annotation procedure do you
    recommend to stay robust to it — or should we correct spillover before
    typing?

Open docs
23. Manufacturing/QC logs (esp. GFP, Ccl2) — done? Share.
24. How do our add-on probes compare to prior custom-probe failures you've seen?
